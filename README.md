### Cash for Cars

`live` &nbsp;QR lead system for folks who buy used cars for cash.

Slap a QR code anywhere: business cards under wipers, flyers, signs, wherever. Someone scans it, fills out a quick form, and the buyer's phone lights up right away.

**The play:** find local car buyers, hook 'em up, bill for the setup.
